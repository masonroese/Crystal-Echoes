  • Each round has two 15-second turns, one per player. The first turn alternates each round.
  • On your turn only your character is live. Every earlier turn, from both players, replays as an echo repeating its exact inputs.
  • Four crystals start each turn: top and bottom of the center line, the top-left corner (Amber's side) and the bottom-right corner (Cobalt's side). Carry them into the center ring. A character can carry several at once and delivers them all together. Deliveries by either side count for that side. A knocked-out carrier drops everything it holds.
  • Catch-up: from round 3 on, a player who is 2 or more crystals behind gets a bonus crystal just inside their gate for that round. 4 or more behind gets 2. Anyone can take them.
  • Scores are banked only at the end of each round, from the second turn. The first turn's deliveries are provisional, because that turn replays as an echo during the second.
  • Turns run the full 15 seconds even after every crystal is delivered, so you can record a route for later rounds. A turn ends early only if every character is knocked out.
  • Before each turn, draft a character you haven't used yet. Trappers can't carry crystals.
  • In the draft you can spend banked crystals to upgrade the character you pick: 1 crystal for Tier 1, 1 more for Tier 2. Spent crystals come off your score. The upgrade also applies to that character's echoes. Each player starts with 2 crystals.
  • Friendly fire applies only to Trapper mines and Frostcaller bolts, which hit your own echoes too. Friendly knockouts don't count toward the tiebreak.
  • Most crystals after the last round wins. Knockouts break ties.
