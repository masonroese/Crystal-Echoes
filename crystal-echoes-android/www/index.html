  • Each round has two 10-second turns, one per player. The first turn alternates each round.
  • On your turn only your character is live. Every earlier turn, from both players, replays as an echo repeating its exact inputs.
  • Every turn, each side sends out 2 runners. They walk from their own gate to the other side along fixed lanes, one along the top and one along the bottom. Both sides use the same lanes, mirrored. Runners carry 1 crystal in rounds 1 and 2, 2 crystals in rounds 3 and 4, and 3 in round 5. A runner that reaches the far side scores 1 point for each crystal it still holds.
  • Runners can't fight back. Each hit makes a runner drop 1 crystal, and the hit that takes its last crystal knocks it out. After a hit, a runner can't be hit again for half a second. Dropped crystals can be picked up by anyone and carried to the center ring for 1 point each. Freezing a runner holds it up, so a runner that is delayed long enough won't make it before the turn ends. Time stops don't affect runners. Ben's shield protects his own runners.
  • One more crystal starts each turn at the top of the center line. Carry it into the center ring for 1 point. A knocked-out carrier drops whatever it holds.
  • Catch-up: from round 4 on, a player who is 4 or more points behind gets 1 bonus crystal just inside their gate for that round. Anyone can take it.
  • Scores are banked only at the end of each round, from the second turn. The first turn's scores are provisional, because that turn replays as an echo during the second.
  • Turns always run the full 10 seconds, so you can record a route for later rounds.
  • Before each turn, draft a character you haven't used yet. Trappers can't carry crystals.
  • In the draft you can spend banked crystals to upgrade the character you pick: 1 crystal for Tier 1, 1 more for Tier 2. Spent crystals come off your score. The upgrade also applies to that character's echoes. Each player starts with 2 crystals.
  • Friendly fire applies only to Trapper mines and Frostcaller bolts, which hit your own echoes too. Friendly knockouts don't count toward the tiebreak.
  • Most crystals after the last round wins. Knockouts break ties.
